Tax Planning & Compliance

We provide complete tax planning and compliance support for salaried professionals, entrepreneurs and SMEs in Pune. Our process focuses on legal tax optimization, timely filing and documentation accuracy.

From ITR filing and GST returns to notice handling and advisory for tax-saving investments, Divyam360 helps you stay compliant while improving overall financial efficiency.

What this service includes

Our tax desk combines practical compliance execution with year-round advisory so you can avoid penalties and improve planning.

  • Income Tax Return (ITR) filing for individuals, freelancers and businesses
  • GST registration, return filing and annual compliance support
  • Advance tax planning and deduction optimization
  • Documentation support for scrutiny, notices and audits
  • Tax-efficient planning linked with insurance and investment goals

  • Who should opt for tax planning support?

    Anyone with salary income, business income, rental income, investments, or multiple deductions can benefit from expert planning and compliance support.

  • Do you handle both tax filing and notice support?

    Yes. We handle end-to-end filing and provide structured support for notices, reconciliations and response documentation.

  • Can tax planning be aligned with investments?

    Absolutely. We align deductions, insurance cover and investment products with your financial goals and tax position.
