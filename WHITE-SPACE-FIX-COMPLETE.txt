MOBILE WHITE SPACE FIX - COMPLETED ✅
=====================================
Date: June 17, 2026

ISSUE FIXED:
White space between sticky header and carousel on mobile screens

ROOT CAUSE:
Two issues were causing the white space:
1. Incorrect margin-top values on .main-slider
2. Default padding-top on .main-slider .swiper-slide .container from main.css

SOLUTION APPLIED:
Updated mobile-sticky-header.css with precise measurements:

MOBILE (≤767px):
- Header height: ~110px (compact)
- .main-slider margin-top: 110px
- .swiper-slide .container padding-top: 90px (reduced from 130px)

TABLET (768-991px):
- Header height: ~140px  
- .main-slider margin-top: 140px
- .swiper-slide .container padding-top: 110px (reduced from 130px)

GENERAL MOBILE (≤991px):
- Header height: ~135px
- .main-slider margin-top: 135px
- .swiper-slide .container padding-top: 130px

RESULT:
✅ No white space between header and carousel
✅ Carousel starts immediately below sticky header
✅ Content visible and properly positioned
✅ Consistent across all mobile breakpoints
✅ Desktop completely unaffected (992px+)
